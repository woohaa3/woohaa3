Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: Square desktop application icon for WooHaa3 (Tauri app, macOS Dock and Windows app icon), transparent background.
Primary request: A near-symbolic version of the brand's isometric miniature with two floating flat UI cards, stripped to the fewest possible elements so it reads as a sign at 32 pixels. No wordmark, no text.

Geometry — the most important rule. True isometric 2.5D with one single consistent projection:
- Orthographic projection. No perspective, no wide angle, no lens distortion.
- Exactly two horizontal axes: one going 30 degrees up to the right, one going 30 degrees up to the left, identical and mirror-symmetric; the vertical axis stays perfectly vertical.
- Every edge of every object is parallel to one of those three axes. No 45 degree edges, no arbitrary slopes.
- The floating slab is a mathematically exact isometric rhombus: both of its lower edges rise at exactly 30 degrees, one to the left and one to the right, so the shape is a perfect mirror-symmetric diamond. Imagine it drawn on a 30-degree isometric grid with a ruler; the left slant and the right slant must be identical, not 31 versus 34 degrees.
- The building sits square to the grid, both visible walls have the same width, its flat roof and parapet follow the two isometric axes, and its centred rooftop block is aligned to the same grid.
- Tree, person and camera stand on the same grid, no object rotated off-axis.

The miniature: an untextured study model on one small floating slab. Exactly four things sit on it: one simple two-storey building with a flat roof and a crisp parapet, one round-canopy tree on a short trunk, one tiny capsule-shaped person, and one tiny video camera on a slim three-leg tripod, placed in front of the building and aimed at it as if recording, with a clearly visible lens and a small round recording light. No annex block, no rooftop block, no second tree, no second camera. Window openings are reduced to exactly one large square per visible wall — two in total, nothing smaller than that.

Colour — exactly two greens taken from the product's own canvas palette, shared by the model AND the cards, neither black nor white:
- Deep jade green #16846c with tonal shading inside the hue (lit #2a9a80, shaded #12755f).
- Warm cream #fdf8ee with tonal shading inside the hue (lit #fffcf7, shaded #efe3d0).
- Distribution: keep the two colours close to balanced, with cream just under half of the miniature and green taking the rest:
  cream: the building walls, the raised parapet band around the flat roof, the top surface of the floating slab, the tree trunk, the camera body and its tripod, and the card bodies;
  jade green: the flat roof deck inside that cream parapet, the two window openings, the tree canopy, the capsule person, the camera lens and its recording light, the extruded side faces and rounded rim of the slab, plus the card borders, placeholder bars, ports and the Bezier connector.
- No black, no white, no pure grey, no third hue; shadows are tinted with the local surface colour.

The floating cards: exactly two flat canvas cards above the building, both perfectly front-facing rounded rectangles, never isometric and never tilted: warm cream surface, thin deep jade border, soft green-tinted shadow, one with a small deep jade image thumbnail placeholder and one short deep jade bar, the other with two short deep jade bars. The two cards are joined only to each other by one smooth 2D Bezier curve with a small circular deep jade port at each end; the curve flows gently between them. No line runs down to the building and nothing connects the cards to the model. Clear air gap so the cards visibly float; they cast a soft shadow on the roof below. One thin jade dashed line also runs from the top of the camera up to the bottom edge of the right-hand card and ends there in a small round jade port; the dashes are even, the line never touches the building, and no other line connects the model to the cards.

Style/medium: contemporary brand identity, two-colour architectural study model rendered as clean icon artwork, matte surfaces, precise geometry, restrained detail. Not glossy plastic, not a toy, not retro 3D, not a photo, not a mockup.
Lighting: one soft key light from the upper left, gentle tinted ambient occlusion, clear tonal separation between lit top faces and shaded side faces, no rim light.
Composition/framing: exactly square. The island with its cards fills about 94 percent of the icon width and height, centred with even small margins, never cropped.
Background: a flat, uniform pure magenta key colour #ff00ff edge to edge, no shadow, no glow, no gradient, so it can be keyed out cleanly. This magenta must appear nowhere inside the artwork and must not tint the model edges. Keep the silhouette closed and crisp.
Constraints: This is the actual icon artwork, not an icon mockup. No text, no wordmark, no letters, no numerals. Exactly one building, one tree, one person, one camera on a tripod and two cards joined by one curved connector — no annex, no rooftop block, no second tree, no second camera, no extra elements. No watermark, no Apple logo, no Tauri logo. No faces on the person.
